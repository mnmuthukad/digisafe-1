About Us

A practical cyber-awareness platform for Kerala, helping every citizen make safer decisions online.

Our purpose

Our Mission

DigiSafe Keralam is an initiative of the Social Engagement Centre, Digital University Kerala, created to make digital safety knowledge practical and accessible to every individual in Kerala. Through clear lessons and real-life examples, people can recognise cyber threats, protect their accounts, and respond with confidence.

How we help

What We Do

Cybersecurity awareness classes

Safe use of online services

Digital learning for senior citizens

Practical learning based on real situations

Accessible content for every level of experience

Responsible and informed digital engagement

Why it matters

Why It Matters

Cyber fraud affects households, students, older adults, and organisations across India. DigiSafe focuses on the practical warning signs and actions that help people pause, verify, and respond safely.
