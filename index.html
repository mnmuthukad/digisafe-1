About the project

Making digital safety a habit for every student in Kerala

Online fraud now reaches students through phones, games, social media and messages from people they trust. Awareness has not kept pace with the risk.

DigiSafe Keralam closes that gap with short, practical lessons built from real scam patterns. Students learn to pause, verify and act, and a simulated scammer shows exactly how pressure and deception work so that they can recognise it when it matters.

The programme is designed and developed by the Social Engagement Centre with a student team at Digital University Kerala, and begins with schools and colleges across the state.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Who is DigiSafe Keralam for?

Students aged 10–19 first, and every learner in Kerala. The lessons use everyday situations, so no technical background is needed.

Who runs the programme?

It is an initiative of the Social Engagement Centre, Digital University Kerala, developed together with a student team.

Which languages are available?

English and Malayalam. Use the language button in the header to switch at any time.

Can I start learning now?

Yes. The intro videos are on the Learn page. The full platform with courses, quizzes and badges is launching soon.

How do I register?

Registration opens with the learning platform. Until then, start with the videos on the Learn page.

What should I do if I have already been scammed?

Stop sending money or codes, tell a trusted adult, call your bank, then call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in.
