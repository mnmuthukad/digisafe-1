The simulated scammer

Introduction to Rogue

Meet Rogue, a simulated scammer built for learning. Rogue acts out the tricks real scammers use so you can recognise them and stay calm. Nothing Rogue does is real.

What you’ll learn

  • Why Rogue exists and how to use the examples
  • The pressure tactics: urgency, false trust and taking control
  • How to pause and verify before you act
Best for: Students aged 10–19
